# Closed testing guide

Google's current requirement for this Personal developer account is **at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days** before applying for production access. Internal testing does not count. Testers do not have to open the app every day, but should genuinely exercise its features and give feedback over the test period. Production access is reviewed rather than automatically granted after the fourteenth day.

Source checked 23 November 2026: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465.

The 12-person/14-day test does not replace the requirement to use the correct developer account type. Google requires Organization registration for financial services: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890. A human support request is pending about the Foundation's specific features and reviewer access. Do not assume that completing a Personal-account test alone permits this app's public release, or that organization verification is a timing workaround.

## Before inviting people

Finish the required app setup, resolve reviewer access and applicable financial-service/account requirements, publish the Closed testing release, and obtain its opt-in link. The current Internal testing URL is not the Closed testing link. The 14-day period has not been established merely by uploading a build or adding email addresses.

Collect the Google-account email addresses that testers use in Google Play on their Android phones. Add those addresses to the Closed testing tester list, save it, and send the actual opt-in link. Do not request Google passwords, OTPs or payment details.

Prefer genuine Foundation members who can review their own accounts. Do not create synthetic production members, contributions, wallet credits, receipts or death events to make the test look populated. Normal membership and contribution rules continue to apply to genuine registrations; test participation does not waive dues. A person who is not a member should arrange legitimate enrollment with the Foundation or use an appropriately isolated review environment, rather than entering invented production details.

## Message the owner can forward once Closed testing is ready

Please help test the Tuluva Mahasabhe Android app. Open **[insert the verified Closed testing opt-in link]** using the Google account you provided, join the test, and install the app from Google Play. Keep that account opted in for 14 consecutive days. Try the app on several days and send feedback about anything confusing or broken. You do not need to make a real payment or use it continuously.

## What to check

- Sign in to your own approved account; close/reopen the app and check that navigation returns to the correct workspace.
- Review profile, membership, plan, dues, wallet and receipts. Empty histories can be valid. Do not initiate real payments simply to test a button; controlled payment acceptance is a separate owner task.
- Read community news and public support events. Test menus, return links and the role-appropriate guide.
- Check your profile photo and downloaded ID card, if applicable. Do not share another person's address, phone, bank information or identity documents.
- Report bugs with Android version, app version, screen, steps, expected result and actual result. Screenshots should hide private information, credentials and codes.
- Use comment reporting/blocking only for genuine moderation needs in production. Exercise artificial reports and sample comments in an isolated environment.

The Foundation should keep a feedback and fix log. Google asks how testers engaged, what feedback was received and what was changed when considering production access. Do not invent participation or feedback, and do not promise approval on a fixed date.
